Privacy Policy

Food Duel Daily Publisher · last updated 7 August 2026

Food Duel Daily Publisher is private software used by Food Duel Daily to publish its own videos to its own social accounts. It has no users other than its operator, no sign-up, and no public interface.

Information we collect from you

None. The software collects no information from members of the public. There are no accounts to create, no forms to submit, and no tracking, analytics, advertising, or cookies on this website.

Information the software handles

  • Our own access token for the Food Duel Daily accounts, obtained when the operator authorizes the application. It is stored locally on the operator's own computer and is used only to publish our own content.
  • Our own videos and captions, created by us, uploaded to our own accounts.
  • Aggregate performance figures for our own posts — views, likes, comments and shares — read back from the platforms to decide what to make next. These are counts about our posts, not information about the individuals who viewed them.

What we do not do

  • We do not access, store, or process the personal data of any other TikTok or Instagram user.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
  • We do not use platform data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not combine platform data with data from other sources.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Credentials and post records live on the operator's own computer, not on a hosted service. Access tokens are deleted when the application's access is revoked. The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's or Instagram's own app settings, which immediately ends the software's ability to act on the account.

Children

The software is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
