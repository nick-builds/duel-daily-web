Food Duel Daily

Two foods enter. You pick a side. New duels daily.

Food Duel Daily publishes short side-by-side food comparisons — regional rivalries, state-fair inventions, and then-versus-now portion changes — to Instagram and TikTok.

About the imagery

The food in these videos is AI-rendered from documented descriptions of real menu items, and every post carries the platform's AI-generated content label. Each duel names its sources: published coverage of the actual dish, so the rendering can be checked against what the item really is.

The publishing tool

Food Duel Daily Publisher is our own internal software. It posts our own original videos to our own accounts on a schedule. It is not offered to other people, has no sign-ups, and handles no one else's data.
